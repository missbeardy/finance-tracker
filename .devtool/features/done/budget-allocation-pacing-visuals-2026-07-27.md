Improve how pool allocation across category groups and pacing indicators (e.g. "113d behind pace") are shown so they're scannable and actionable.

## Acceptance criteria
- Allocation across category groups is visually clear at phone width.
- Pacing indicators explain meaning, not just a cryptic number.
- One separation method per surface (hairline or shadow) per design rules.
